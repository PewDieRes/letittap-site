It works the way you do: one tap at a time.

There are no special connections to other apps. LetItTap looks at what is on the screen, picks the next tap or bit of typing, does it, then looks again to check it worked. That is why it can work in almost any app.

  • The bubble stays bright yellow so you can always spot it over any app. It turns red while it’s working; tap it to stop.
  • You see every step in a line of text under the bubble.
  • Most everyday jobs take between 3 and 15 steps.
  • Ask out loud or type, whichever is easier at the time.
  • Tap the bubble while it works and it stops straight away.

What it can see, and what it won’t do

LetItTap needs Android’s Accessibility permission to read the screen and tap for you. Here is exactly how that permission is used.

It only acts when you ask
Nothing happens until you start a task from the bubble. When the task ends or you tap stop, it stops reading the screen.
Text, not screenshots
During a task, the text on the current screen is sent to the AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) to choose the next step. No images are taken.
No accounts, no servers of ours
LetItTap talks to the AI model directly from your phone. We don’t run servers, and we don’t keep a copy of anything you do.
No payments, deleting or passwords
It refuses to pay, buy, delete or type passwords. When a task needs one of those, it stops and tells you.
